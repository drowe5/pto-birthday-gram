Orders

Drop the export CSV or TSV · or click to choose

In Cheddar Up, open the collection, then Reports → Export and choose the item-level report so each gram is its own row. If you only have an .xlsx, open it and Save as → CSV first.

Start at /v1/collections to find your birthday gram collection, then follow its id into the endpoint that lists payments or items. Whatever comes back, the rows below get mapped the same way.
