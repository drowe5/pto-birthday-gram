Orders

Drop the Cheddar Up export .xlsx, .csv or .tsv · or click to choose

In Cheddar Up open the collection and choose Reports → Export. The workbook it gives you has a sheet per item; the birthday gram sheet is the one with a column per checkout question, and it is picked automatically.

Grams

Click a name to see that card, or its tick to leave that one out. Hovering a row offers ⤒ and ⤓: exclude everything above it, or include it and everything below — which is how a batch gets printed up to a date and carried on from there next time.
